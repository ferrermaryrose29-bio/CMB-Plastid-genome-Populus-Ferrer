## Report Summary

- **Organism:** *Populus trichocarpa* (black cottonwood), family Salicaceae
- **Accession:** NC_009143.1 (RefSeq)
- **Genome size:** 157,033 bp, circular
- **GC content:** 36.68%
- **Structure:** LSC 85,128 bp, SSC 16,599 bp, IR 27,653 bp (each copy)
- **Gene features:** 144 (98 CDS, 37 tRNA, 8 rRNA, 1 pseudogene), counting both IR copies
